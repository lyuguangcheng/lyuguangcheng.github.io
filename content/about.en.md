# About me

I am someone who likes *making the thing*. Writing code is not just about meeting a requirement — it is how a vague idea gradually becomes clear, because most problems only really surface once you start building.

My day-to-day interests are front-end engineering, data visualisation and developer experience. I like restrained, clean interfaces, structures you can grasp at a glance, and small touches that save the user one click.

## Experience

- **Independent developer** (2026 — present) · self-directed projects and long-term maintenance of this site
- **Data visualisation intern** (2025) · dashboards and metric definitions
- **Undergraduate** (2022 — 2026) · computer science

## What I care about

- **Readability first** — code is written for people, and incidentally executed by machines
- **Make it run, then optimise** — optimisation without real feedback is usually self-indulgence
- **Treat edge cases as the real work** — empty data, slow networks and small screens are where users actually are
